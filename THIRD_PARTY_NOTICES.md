## Data sources

The ingestion pipeline downloads this data at run time. The repository contains only small test fixtures taken from real responses (`ingest/tests/fixtures/`), which are credited here as well.

| Source | Used for | Terms | Credit shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Launch Library 2](https://thespacedevs.com/llapi), The Space Devs | Launches, providers, vehicles, pads, missions, boosters and landings | No license or attribution requirement is published | Launch data: The Space Devs, Launch Library 2 (thespacedevs.com) |
| [CelesTrak](https://celestrak.org) | Satellite catalog (SATCAT) and orbital elements (GP) | No attribution requirement is stated; the data is freely available | Orbital data: CelesTrak (celestrak.org) |
| [NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics](https://ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov) | Close approaches (SBDB Close Approach API) and impact risk (Sentry API) | No requirement is stated | Small-body data: NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics (SBDB Close Approach and Sentry APIs) |
| [NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov) | Kp index, X-ray flares, alerts, solar wind, aurora forecast (OVATION) | NOAA data is open; NOAA asks for attribution and forbids implying endorsement or affiliation | Space weather data: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC) |
| [NASA Exoplanet Archive](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu) | Confirmed planets (`pscomppars` table, DOI 10.26133/NEA13) | Public NASA data; the archive asks for its acknowledgment | This research has made use of the NASA Exoplanet Archive, which is operated by the California Institute of Technology, under contract with the National Aeronautics and Space Administration under the Exoplanet Exploration Program. |

Per-source details (endpoints, rate limits, update cadence, quirks): [docs/sources/](docs/sources/).

## Visuals

Every image in `docs/media/` is a screenshot of Hoshizora's own interface. The starfield, nebula and planet are procedural shaders written for this project. No photography, agency logos or artwork from films, anime or games are used.
